# Sources and reading guide

Prepared 5 September 2026. Primary sources and original educators were selected for clarity, practical usefulness and sequence—not a claim of a universal 'best' ranking. Use selected sections rather than entire courses. Dates are recorded only when available; documentation evolves. Optional model/cloud execution can cost money even when reading is freely accessible. Enterprise accounts below are first-party reports, with their stated limitations.
